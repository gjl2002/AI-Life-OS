# Moments Context Verification

## Purpose

朋友圈 is short, but it is high-trust. Before final output in enhanced, strict, or campaign mode, verify that every credibility-bearing sentence is supported by a real source, user-provided material, or a clearly weakened expression.

This pass prevents the common failure where a post sounds smooth but quietly invents personal scenes, customer proof, product facts, or conversion claims.

## Verification Pass

Before final drafts, build this internal map:

```markdown
事实来源：
- 个人场景来源：
- 客户/私域证据来源：
- 产品/CTA来源：
- 知识/观点来源：
- 公开/书籍来源：
- 过往朋友圈差异化：

未验证但影响信任的点：
- ...

需要弱化/删除的句子：
- ...

可放心写的内容：
- ...
```

## Claim Rules

- First-person story requires user-owned material or user-provided scene.
- Customer feedback/result requires customer/private-domain evidence or user-provided proof.
- Product promise, price, quota, deadline, and报名方式 require current product/offer evidence.
- Benchmarks and public examples can inspire structure, but cannot become the user's experience.
- Knowledge models can support explanation, but should not be presented as lived proof.
- Public studies, books, podcasts, interviews, talks, and reports need a full working source record. The post needs a natural source anchor; a full reference block is optional and should appear only when reader trust requires it.
- For enhanced, strict, and campaign posts, confirm the new post has a distinct scene, proof, emotion, consequence, stage, or CTA role from similar past朋友圈.
- If publicability is unclear, anonymize, blur, generalize, or ask.

## Weaken Instead Of Invent

If verification is incomplete:
- Replace "很多客户都..." with "最近我在几个交流里看到..."
- Replace "已经帮很多人..." with "我正在把这件事做得更可复制..."
- Replace "效果很好" with "至少这个反馈让我确认..."
- Replace exact price, deadline, or quota with "如果你想了解，我可以私聊发你".

## Final Decision

Only deliver a final post when:
- the main scene/proof is real.
- the CTA matches verified product/current offer.
- private or sensitive details are not exposed.
- unsupported claims have been removed or softened.
