# Trend Momentum

Use this conditional module only when external timing, platform, ecosystem, industry, policy, or technology momentum may change the opportunity decision.

## Point-Line-Surface-Body Table

```markdown
点:
  具体产品/功能/服务:
  当前可交付性:
线:
  长期发展路径:
  增长/衰退信号:
面:
  平台/生态/行业/渠道/资源:
  谁在赋能:
体:
  时代趋势/经济体:
  上升/停滞/下降:
势:
  顺势 / 平推 / 逆势:
敌人:
  对手竞争 / 趋势变化 / 结构错位 / 自身交付:
```

## Judgment Rules

- Start from the point the user can actually deliver, but do not stop at point-level excitement.
- A good opportunity should borrow from a growing line, useful surface, or rising body.
- If the real enemy is trend change, direct competitor analysis is not enough.
- When platforms/ecosystems compete, a small product point may receive leverage from the surface.
- Use `求之于势，不责于人`: if the product needs huge persuasion or constant willpower from users, check whether the scene and trend are wrong.

## High/Low Momentum Rewrite

When useful, rewrite:

```markdown
低势能版本:
- 点:
- 线:
- 面:
- 体:

高势能版本:
- 点:
- 线:
- 面:
- 体:

机会升级:
```

Connect the opportunity to the user's current `commercial_positioning` and actual product path. Do not embed a permanent strategic direction or personal product vocabulary in this model.

Trend is leverage, not proof of demand. A rising market cannot compensate for an unknown first user, absent trigger scene, weak switch reason, or unproven delivery.
