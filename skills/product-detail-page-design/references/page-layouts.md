# Product Detail Page Layouts

Choose the smallest sequence that can carry the verified sales argument. Do not fill a fixed number of images when the evidence is thin.

## Four-Image Sequence

Use for a clear, low-complexity offer:

1. **Hero** — target user, current problem, credible promise, product identity.
2. **Fit And Mechanism** — who it is for, why the old approach fails, how this offer works.
3. **Delivery And Outcome** — stages, deliverables, experience, proof, boundaries.
4. **Offer And CTA** — package, price, risk reducer, fit/not-fit, next action.

## Seven-Image Sequence

Use for a higher-consideration or structurally complex offer:

1. Hero promise and product identity.
2. User conflict, failed substitutes, and problem reframing.
3. Unique mechanism and product path.
4. Audience fit, use scenes, and user-owned outcomes.
5. Delivery blueprint, service experience, or system architecture.
6. Curriculum, modules, timeline, or implementation steps.
7. Offer stack, verified proof, price, risk reducer, and CTA.

## Module Rules

Each image should perform one sales job:

- make the right buyer recognize themselves;
- explain a mechanism;
- show a concrete deliverable;
- reduce a specific objection;
- provide verified proof;
- clarify fit and non-fit;
- make the next action obvious.

Avoid pages that merely repeat features, decorative slogans, unverified numbers, or the same promise in different words.

## Responsive Composition

For tall mobile-first pages:

- one dominant message per screen segment;
- short body copy;
- large type hierarchy;
- enough whitespace around evidence;
- diagrams readable without zooming;
- CTA repeated only after a new reason to act;
- critical Chinese text added with an accurate text/compositing layer when generation quality is unreliable.

## Reference Use

When the user supplies visual references, extract information hierarchy, module order, density, whitespace, typography scale, evidence treatment, diagram style, and CTA rhythm.

Do not reproduce proprietary text, logo, personal image, watermark, testimonial, price, screenshot, or exact composition.
