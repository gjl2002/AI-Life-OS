# Reader Flow Pass

Use this after paragraph-level revision and before the final review. This is a mandatory quality gate for full WeChat articles.

The goal is not to make the article sound smoother. The goal is to make the reader understand why the article moves from one paragraph to the next.

## Core Standard

A publishable article should not only have correct paragraphs. It should create forward motion.

Each paragraph must do one of these:
- answer a clear reader question
- raise the next natural question
- add a new scene, contradiction, evidence, action, or consequence
- move the article from one lens to another with an explicit reason

If two paragraphs explain the same judgment without a new question, scene, contrast, evidence, or action, merge or cut one.

## Paragraph Flow Audit

Before final output, audit the draft paragraph by paragraph.

For each paragraph, identify:
- answered question: what question this paragraph answers
- next reader question: what the reader naturally wants to know next
- next paragraph connection: whether the next paragraph catches that question
- movement type: progression, turn, explanation, example, scene shift, framework shift, or conclusion
- repetition risk: whether this paragraph repeats the same function or judgment as another paragraph

Use this compact working table when useful:

```markdown
段落：
回答的问题：
读完会问：
下一段是否接住：
推进类型：
是否重复：
处理：保留 / 压缩 / 合并 / 移动 / 重写 / 删除
```

Do not include the full table in the final response unless the user asks for the review trail or the article needed substantial structural repair.

## Required Flow Checks

The article must pass these checks before final delivery:

1. Opening to definition: if the article starts with a strong judgment, it must quickly answer what the key term or hidden problem actually means.
2. Definition to difficulty: after explaining the key idea, it must explain why people still fail even after knowing it.
3. Public problem to personal scene: if a personal scene appears, the transition must explain why the writer's experience is relevant here.
4. Scene to framework: if the article moves from story to model, the transition must extract the model from the scene.
5. Framework to action: if the article gives a framework, it must show what the reader can do differently next.
6. Action to product bridge: if a product appears, it must grow from an unresolved reader problem, not appear as a sudden ad.
7. Ending: the ending must return to the deeper judgment, not merely summarize the article.

## Bridge Types

Use bridges only when they clarify why the next paragraph exists. Do not decorate the article with transition phrases.

### Explanation Bridge

Use when moving from a judgment to a definition or mechanism.

Example:

> 但问题来了，很多人知道要有上下文，却不知道上下文到底是什么。

### Scene Bridge

Use when moving from a public problem to the user's own experience.

Example:

> 至少我自己就是这样。

### Progression Bridge

Use when moving from knowing something to the harder problem of doing it.

Example:

> 知道这一点还不够，真正难的是把它留在系统里。

### Framework Bridge

Use when moving from scattered observations to a structured model.

Example:

> 如果一定要把 AI 学习分成几层，我会这样理解。

## Anti-Pattern

Avoid bridge words that only create a fake sense of flow:
- 更重要的是
- 说到底
- 另外
- 接下来
- 总结一下
- 归根结底

These are acceptable only when they clearly answer:

> I just explained A. Why must I now explain B?

## Repair Moves

When the article feels like it is circling the same point, use one of these repairs:

- Compress repeated explanation into one sharp sentence.
- Turn the next paragraph into the reader's next question.
- Insert a concrete scene before another abstract explanation.
- Move the framework earlier if readers need a map.
- Move the personal scene earlier if readers need trust.
- Add an action paragraph if the article keeps diagnosing without release.
- Cut a paragraph if it has no new question, scene, turn, evidence, or action.

## Pass Criteria

The article can be delivered only when:
- every paragraph answers or raises a distinct question
- each paragraph's ending naturally creates the next paragraph's need
- no two adjacent paragraphs explain the same judgment in different words only
- major lens shifts have a reason, not just a transition phrase
- the reader can feel the article moving from problem to cause to scene to framework to action or value judgment

If the article fails this pass, revise the structure before title design.
