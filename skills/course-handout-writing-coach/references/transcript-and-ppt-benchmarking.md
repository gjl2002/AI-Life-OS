# Transcript and PPT Benchmarking / 对标语料与 PPT 拆解

Use benchmark material to learn mechanisms, not to reproduce proprietary expression.

## Transcript Deconstruction

For each useful segment, record:

| Segment role | Learner scene | Old interpretation | Causal mechanism | Teaching move | Action / asset | Transfer rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Also observe delivery choices: sentence length, question placement, contrast, example timing, depth of explanation, and transitions. Never copy wording, stories, numerical claims, course sequences, or named learner cases.

## PPT Deconstruction

For each page, record: page job, screen sentence, visual type, spoken expansion, evidence/demo, and transition. Identify what is deliberately absent from the screen. A strong deck usually gives the audience one cognitive job per page while the speaker supplies the causal detail.

## Output

Turn the analysis into a `可迁移教学机制库`, for example: `场景 → 误解 → 反直觉判断 → 因果拆解 → 对比案例 → 方法 → 工具 → 作业`. Do not turn it into a phrase bank.
