# Note Empathy Radar

Use this reference before title design, cover selection, opening, image order, and CTA. Xiaohongshu notes need fast emotional recognition: the reader should feel "this is about me" before they evaluate the whole argument.

Do not use this as proof by itself. Apply it only to real image evidence, user scenes, comments, feedback, product proof, or verified reader/avatar insights.

## Working Table

```markdown
| Evidence / image | First-impression emotion | Reader need / fear | Title or cover job | Body / CTA response | Defense risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Use this table internally for enhanced, strict, campaign, product, milestone, or claim-heavy notes. For quick notes, a one-line diagnosis is enough.

## Platform Emotion Jobs

### 焦虑 -> title and first screen

Use when the reader is afraid of missing out, choosing wrong, falling behind, or wasting effort.

Title job:
- name the specific uncertainty.
- make the reader feel recognized, not threatened.

Avoid:
- fake urgency.
- broad fear claims without evidence.

### 不爽 -> hook and body tension

Use when the reader is tired of a common method, tool, routine, or social expectation.

Opening job:
- name the friction in ordinary language.
- show one concrete image/scene that proves it.

Avoid:
- starting with abstract methodology.

### 爽 -> cover/result reveal

Use when a visual or story shows a clear release moment, milestone, finished system, first result, or "finally done" feeling.

Cover job:
- make the result instantly inspectable.
- pair with a title that explains why it matters.

Avoid:
- unsupported result promises.

### 羞耻 -> protection and tone

Use when the reader may feel embarrassed about not knowing, not starting, being disorganized, or needing help.

Tone job:
- speak as "I also went through this" or "this is a common hidden problem."
- reduce exposure risk with practical next steps.

Avoid:
- mocking, superiority, or "你还不会..." framing.

### 防御 -> low-pressure CTA

Use when a note mentions product, class, consultation, private chat, or strong advice.

CTA job:
- invite comment, keyword, checklist, diagnosis, or a small next step.
- make product mention feel earned by evidence.

Avoid:
- hard-sell endings.
- asking for private chat before trust is built.

### 愤怒 -> boundary and trust repair

Use when the audience has been hurt by bad products, fake screenshots, overpromising, or manipulative courses.

Trust job:
- show real process, limits, and what is not promised.
- avoid exaggerated lifestyle or income claims.

## Title / Cover Checks

Before finalizing titles:
- Does the title name a real emotion entry instead of a generic benefit?
- Does the cover image prove the same thing the title promises?
- Does the opening continue the title's emotional contract?
- Does any title shame the reader or make unsupported fear claims?

Before finalizing image order:
- Put the strongest evidence or scene first.
- Put context before proof when the image is confusing.
- Put product/conversion image after trust has been built.
- Omit images that create privacy, publicability, or claim mismatch risk.

## Guardrails

- Do not create shame for clicks.
- Do not let "焦虑" become fear selling.
- Do not use a big claim if images only show a small process.
- Do not compress a full WeChat argument into a note; keep one scene, one judgment, one useful action.
