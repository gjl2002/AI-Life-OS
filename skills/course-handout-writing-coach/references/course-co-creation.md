# Course Co-creation / 课程共创

Use this before deep retrieval when the course is not yet a stable production object.

## Freeze a Course Contract

| Field | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Target learner | Who is entering, in what concrete starting condition? |
| Transformation | What can they understand, do, or possess by the end? |
| First principle | What causal judgment holds the course together? |
| Boundary | What is explicitly outside the promise? |
| Delivery | Live / recorded / workshop; cadence; duration; feedback channel |
| Course assets | What asset is built, used, updated, or reviewed across lessons? |

## Lesson-role Map

Every lesson needs a unique job. Do not create a directory of topics.

| Lesson | Learner obstacle | Changed judgment | Incoming asset | Outgoing asset | Next use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Concept Distribution

For recurring concepts, decide where they are introduced, explained deeply, practiced, and reused. A later lesson must apply or deepen a concept rather than repeat its introduction.

| Concept | First recognition | Deep explanation | Practice | Review / reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Stop full drafting if the course cannot explain why its lessons must happen in this order.
