# Handoff Rules

Use this reference to decide what should happen before or after lesson writing.

## Back To Product Architecture

Return to `product-architecture-coach` when:

- the course promise is unclear
- lesson order is unstable
- the module has no deliverable
- the lesson goal changes depending on target user
- writing would hide a structural problem

Provide:

- unclear lesson/module
- missing architecture decision
- proposed question to resolve

## To Product Experience

Use `product-experience-coach` after lesson writing when:

- assignments require cohort cadence
- feedback workload is unclear
- students may drop off during submission
- the product needs onboarding, activation, retention, or peak/end design

Provide:

- lesson tasks
- submission rules
- feedback rubric
- expected student burden
- likely drop-off point

## To Detail Page

Use `product-detail-page-design` after lesson writing when:

- handouts, assignments, and deliverables prove what the product includes
- the user wants to turn delivery details into buyer-facing value

Provide:

- actual lesson outcomes
- deliverables
- assignments
- proof limits
- what can be claimed truthfully

## To Next Lesson

When the user is writing a full course, recommend writing one lesson at a time unless:

- the lessons are short practice notes
- the user asks only for lesson briefs
- the full module is already stable
