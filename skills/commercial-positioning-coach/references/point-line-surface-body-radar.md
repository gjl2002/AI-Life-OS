# Point-Line-Surface-Body Radar

Use this radar when evaluating a life direction, career move, product direction, content direction, offer, or major project. The goal is to judge whether the direction can borrow momentum from a growing structure, not only whether the current point can be executed.

Core formula:

```text
机会价值 = 点的可行性 x 线的增长性 x 面的赋能度 x 体的上升性 x 势能位置
```

## Judgment Rules

- Start from a stable point, but never stop at the point. A good point should be able to enter a longer line.
- Do not reward only effort. A hard-working point in a declining line or wrong surface may still be low value.
- Separate opponent risk from trend risk. If the real enemy is a changing body or surface, beating a direct competitor may not solve the problem.
- Look for `面打架，点有红利`: when platforms, ecosystems, or industries compete to empower the same user behavior, the small point may gain leverage.
- Use `求之于势，不责于人`: if execution feels unusually hard, first check whether the direction is pushing against structure.
- Prefer direction upgrades over identity decoration. A high-energy version should show a clearer line, surface, body, and momentum path, not just a better name.

## Evidence Discipline

Label each strategic claim:

- `confirmed`: supported by repeated behavior, records, user/customer evidence, market signal, product signal, or public trend evidence.
- `reasonable insight`: supported by partial evidence and coherent with the user's records.
- `hypothesis`: promising but not yet proven.

Do not present a trend as confirmed when only the user's enthusiasm supports it. Turn missing evidence into validation tasks.

## Output Guidance

For final positioning work, include one opportunity structure table, one low/high energy contrast when the current direction is too small, one decision recommendation, and 3-5 validation tasks if the conclusion is not confirmed.
