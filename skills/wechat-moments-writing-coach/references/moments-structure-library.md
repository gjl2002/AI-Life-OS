# Moments Structure Library

## Content Types And Question Flows

### 人设生活

Purpose: show a real person and build emotional trust.

Ask:
- Where were you and what happened?
- What detail still feels vivid?
- What emotion or state do you want people to feel?
- What sentence do you want to say but have not said?

Structure:
1. Time/place/scene.
2. One concrete detail.
3. Small feeling or realization.
4. Short closing line.

Tone: relaxed, concrete, intimate, not theatrical.

### 专业价值

Purpose: show judgment, competence, and method without hard selling.

Ask:
- What did you do today?
- What problem did you notice or solve?
- What does this show about your method or ability?
- What should the reader understand after seeing this?

Structure:
1. Concrete case or scene.
2. What I saw/did/thought.
3. Insight or method.
4. Optional soft CTA.

Tone: clear, sincere, independent, not lecturing.

### 营销卖货

Purpose: guide consultation, signup, purchase, or action.

Ask:
- What product or service is being promoted?
- What pain or desire does it address?
- Who is it for and not for?
- What proof exists?
- What deadline, quota, price, bonus, or next action matters?

Structure:
1. Hook: phenomenon, pain, emotion, or specific result.
2. User situation.
3. Product/solution and why it exists.
4. Proof: feedback, result, process, or social proof.
5. Scarcity or reason to act now.
6. CTA.

Tone: direct and warm. Like telling a true story that naturally leads to action.

### 概念解释

Purpose: explain a concept, model, method, distinction, or thinking tool in朋友圈 language.

Ask progressively:
1. What concept do you want to explain?
2. How would you explain it to a friend in one sentence?
3. Why does it matter to you?
4. What is it like?
5. What example makes it obvious?
6. What is it not?
7. How can someone use it?

Structure:
1. One-sentence explanation.
2. Why it matters.
3. Analogy.
4. Example.
5. How to use it.
6. Memorable closing line.

### 过程记录

Purpose: record an experience, creation process, emotional change, or growth arc.

Ask:
- When did this happen and why record it now?
- What were the phases and turning points?
- Which moment hit you hardest?
- What image, sound, action, or sentence do you still remember?
- Do you want the ending to be resolved, open, or still-in-progress?

Structure:
1. Opening conflict or realization.
2. Timeline in 2-4 beats.
3. Emotional high point.
4. Concrete detail.
5. Current understanding or open question.

## Default Output

- 默认只输出一个符合用户选定类型的最终稿。
- 自然口语、专业清醒或转化加强是正文内部的语气选择，不是默认输出多个版本的理由。
- 只有用户明确要求对比或多个选项时，才输出变体。
- 默认按完整语义分段：同一个场景、例子或推理留在同一段，一般每段 2-4 句。
- 单句成段只用于少量开头、转折、强调或收尾，不要把整篇拆成一句一段。
- 段落节奏优先参考当前账号已发布内容或用户指定的风格资料。

For life posts:
- 松弛版, 走心版, 有态度版.

For sales posts:
- 温和私域版, 强钩子版, 发售冲刺版.

## Length Guide

- Ordinary post: 100-300 Chinese characters.
- Long朋友圈: 300-600 Chinese characters.
- Pinned post or launch origin story: 500-900 Chinese characters when needed.
- Campaign sequence: each post should still feel like a朋友圈 post, not a公众号 section.

## CTA Options

- No CTA: life/persona and pure trust building.
- Soft CTA: "想了解可以私信我", "评论区我放一下", "有类似情况可以来聊聊".
- Direct CTA: "想报名私信我", "今晚截止", "还有 X 个名额".
- Screening CTA: "适合 X，不适合 Y".

Use no CTA when a post's role is only 认识我 or 理解我.
