# New-Species Sales Expression

Use this when a sales page needs to explain that a product is not merely an old product plus AI, automation, community, commerce, IP, data, or another feature.

## Core Rule

```text
销售页不要只说“加了新功能”，要讲清楚新要素如何改变产品底层逻辑。
```

## Expression Checks

Before writing the page, identify:

```markdown
旧产品是什么:
用户以为的新功能是什么:
真正改变的底层逻辑是什么:
用户前台看到什么:
后台新增了什么资源/流程/反馈:
用户最终拥有的结果为什么不同:
```

## Page Placement

- **Hero**: name the new category/result, not only the old product type.
- **Cognitive conflict**: show why the old product logic fails.
- **Failed path**: explain why old tools plus more effort do not compound.
- **Unique mechanism**: show the new loop created by the new element.
- **System blueprint**: visualize the changed resource/feedback structure.
- **Outcomes**: translate the new logic into owned results.

## AI Life-System Copy Lens

Weak:

```text
Notion 模板 + AI 提示词，帮你更快写目标和复盘。
```

Stronger:

```text
真实记录成为上下文，AI 成为理解引擎，复盘成为持续反馈机制。
```

Use the stronger frame only when the product actually supports durable records, context accumulation, recurring review, and action follow-up. Otherwise mark the claim as `[待确认]`.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim "新物种" just because the product uses AI.
- Do not hide a normal course/template behind inflated category language.
- Do not make the mechanism too abstract for a buyer to understand.
- Do not promise transformation without showing the concrete system, artifact, loop, or support process.
