# Evidence and Claim Discipline / 证据与主张纪律

## Evidence Hierarchy

| Type | Can support | Cannot support | Suggested wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary research / official documentation | a defined mechanism or technical condition | universal learner outcome | `这说明……条件会影响……` |
| Established model / book | a reusable conceptual lens | proof that this cohort will change | `这个模型提供了一个理解角度……` |
| 用户本人真实实践 | a bounded scene, decision, failure, or repair | general effectiveness | `在我的实践里，我观察到……` |
| User language / consultation | a real learner friction or vocabulary | why a method works | `很多人会这样描述……` |
| Benchmark course | internal teaching mechanism and delivery pattern | outward-facing proof or copied content | never cite as authority without permission |
| Hypothesis | a testable next action | fact or proof | `一个值得验证的解释是……` |

## Theory-in-context Test

Before naming a theory or source, ask: does it make the learner understand a causal mechanism more accurately or make a better decision? If not, retain it in the internal ledger only.

If it is named, first explain the learner's scene and mechanism in ordinary language; then optionally attach the name in one sentence. Do not use a standalone bibliography slide as a substitute for teaching.
